Chart color override · Step 4 (Style) of Chart Builder

User can change the chart color. To stay consistent in both modes, we expose a curated 12-swatch picker (not a free hex input). All swatches sit at the 700 shade, all pass AA contrast in light + dark.

Why curated, not free hex? A free color picker can produce dark-mode contrast failures, which violates the §1 principle "errors prevented, not corrected." All 12 swatches are pre-validated for both modes. Free hex stays available as a V2 enhancement once we add a contrast-check guard.
Out of scope for V1 (parking lot)
  • Free hex / HSL color picker (V2 — pair with live contrast guard)
  • Saving a custom palette across charts (V2 — needs persistence layer)
  • Per-bar color override in a bar chart (V2 — needs multi-color editor UI)
  • Color-blind safe mode toggle (V2 — picks alternative palette)
